Coming around the holidays ‘26…a new album, “Abbey’s Plant.” Our late kitty Abbey used to sit in our old basement by the warm furnace, and next to her fave spot grew up through a crack in the cement floor a small, pale plant. The songs I wrote and sang during COVID were like that plant—weird, fighting to be seen, affected and yet unaffected by the outside world. We’ll let you know as soon as the plant is fully grown. xo RT
